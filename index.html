Our Story

The Story Behind Velora Dune

Velora Dune is a contemporary dining destination inspired by the landscapes of Arabia and the flavors of the Mediterranean. The name itself is a quiet tribute to the golden dunes that meet the city's glass towers — two worlds, one table.

Located in the heart of Downtown Dubai, our restaurant combines traditional cooking techniques with modern culinary innovation, set within a room of matte black stone, brushed brass, and low emerald light.

Every dish is created using premium ingredients sourced from around the world — Mediterranean olive oil, Omani saffron, Wagyu beef, and herbs grown on our own rooftop garden — and finished tableside whenever the story of a dish calls for it.

Executive Chef

Chef Adrian Khalil

15 Years of Culinary Craft, Between Paris and the Gulf

Chef Adrian Khalil brings over 15 years of international culinary experience, combining French techniques with Middle Eastern heritage. Trained in Lyon and shaped by kitchens across Beirut and Dubai, his cooking bridges precision and warmth.

At Velora Dune, Chef Khalil leads a kitchen built around seasonality, restraint, and a deep respect for where each ingredient comes from — letting saffron, dates, and charcoal speak for themselves.
